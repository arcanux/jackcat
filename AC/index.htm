About This Site

I originally designed this site with information I began compiling in Beta for the enjoyment and education of my wonderful vassal, Ned Cleversun. I must have done an excellent job because once he began to play, he immediately surpassed me in level and is now eight TWENTY-NINE levels above me. He is nice enough to dump points regularly into loyalty, so I don't fall too far behind! I finally decided that the site was just far too nice to keep to myself.

Once more, I will give fair warning: this site contains *SPOILERS*.
